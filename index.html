INNER SECRETS OF ICE AND FIRE

An illustrated investigation into the hidden anatomy, astrology, tarot, and symbolic geography of the world of Ice and Fire.
What if there is a whole other level to Game of Thrones that you never imagined?

Did you realize that the map of Westeros contains the key anatomical features of a human body? The North forms the head and face — with an eye, nose, mouth, chin, and neck — and the pattern continues downward from there.

A Hidden Body Beneath the Map

Inner Secrets of Ice and Fire is an independent, heavily illustrated exploration of hidden anatomical and astrological structures within Westeros and Essos. The main book is planned as a richly visual project of 500+ pages, with diagrams, maps, symbolic analysis, and related poster, tarot, and medical-astrology material.

The body-map thesis extends through the zodiacal sequence of the traditional Zodiacal Man.

Astrology, Houses, and Symbolic Correspondences

The project traces repeated correspondences through signs, landscapes, castles, houses, animals, characters, and story symbolism. One vivid example is the Leo / Lannister pattern, with its layered solar and leonine imagery.

Examples from the planned twelve-poster zodiac series.

Tarot and the Deeper Symbolic Layer

Beyond anatomy and astrology, the project also explores a deeper hidden layer involving the Major Arcana of tarot and their symbolic placement within the world of Ice and Fire.
