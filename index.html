INNER SECRETS OF ICE AND FIRE

An illustrated investigation into the hidden anatomy, astrology, tarot and symbolic geography of the world of Ice and Fire.
What if there is a whole other level to Game of Thrones that you never imagined?

Did you realize that the map of Westeros contains the key anatomical features of a human body? The North forms the head and face — with an eye, nose, mouth, chin and neck — and the pattern continues downward from there.

Even more strikingly, the twelve astrological signs run through this continental body in their traditional anatomical order, reinforced through geography, houses, castles, animals, characters, mythology and story events.

A Hidden Body Beneath the Map

Inner Secrets of Ice and Fire is an independent, heavily illustrated exploration of proposed anatomical and astrological structures within Westeros and Essos. The project follows correspondences from Aries and the head through the traditional Zodiacal Man, while also investigating a deeper layer involving the Major Arcana of tarot.

The principal Kickstarter project is developing into a large digital book containing hundreds of pages, maps, diagrams and illustrations, together with related poster, tarot and medical-astrology material.
